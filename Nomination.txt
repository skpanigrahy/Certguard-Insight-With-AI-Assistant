Project / Initiative Overview
As enterprise applications continue to grow in scale and complexity, certificate-related issues remain one of the most difficult operational risks to identify before they impact production services. The team transformed CertGuardian Neo from a legacy monitoring solution into a modern, scalable platform that provides continuous visibility into deployed client and server certificates, enabling engineering teams to proactively identify certificate risks before they affect business-critical applications.
Today, CertGuardian Neo provides real-time visibility across more than 35,000 deployed certificates, supporting 526 applications spanning 93 products. By surfacing certificate expiry risks, trust chain issues, and runtime configuration anomalies, the platform enables faster investigation, informed operational decisions, and improved service resilience.
The team re-architected the platform with enhanced dashboards, intelligent search, advanced analytics, and a scalable design to significantly improve usability and long-term sustainability. To accelerate enterprise adoption, the team independently integrated CertGuardian Neo into the enterprise application framework after taking ownership of the implementation, delivering a seamless user experience without delaying delivery. The platform was also enhanced with readiness for emerging post-quantum cryptography standards, ensuring it remains prepared for future security requirements.
Delivered through close collaboration with engineering, infrastructure, cybersecurity, and application teams, CertGuardian Neo has become a strategic enterprise capability that strengthens operational resilience, improves runtime certificate visibility, accelerates issue detection and resolution, and establishes a scalable foundation for future innovation.
